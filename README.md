# Hi, I'm Asif Marazi 👋

**Researcher · Climate Change · Cryosphere & Hydrology · Remote Sensing / GIS · Geospatial Data Analysis**<br>
University of Kashmir, Srinagar

I study how climate change affects water resources in the Himalaya, especially **snow cover, glaciers and streamflow**. My main tools are **remote sensing** and **GIS**, along with Python-based geospatial data analysis and machine learning.

---

### 🔬 Research interests
- Climate change and its impacts on Himalayan water resources
- Snow-cover monitoring and mapping
- Glacier change monitoring using remote sensing
- Streamflow and hydrological response to a changing climate
- Environmental remote sensing applications (land surface temperature, land use, coastal ecosystems)

**Keywords:** climate change · cryosphere · snow · glaciers · hydrology · water resources · remote sensing · GIS · Himalaya
